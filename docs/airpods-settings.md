# AirPods settings

A list of settings to consider adding later. None of them are planned yet.

Most of these are stored on the AirPods, and changing them uses Apple's private protocol. Windows has no public way to reach it, so they would need a driver, such as MagicAAP. Check this per setting before building one, because this list only records what Apple offers.

"Pro" means AirPods Pro 1, 2 and 3. "AirPods 4" means the model without Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), and "AirPods 5" means both versions.

## Already in the app

These run in the app rather than on the AirPods, so they don't need a driver.

| Setting | Models |
|---|---|
| Ear detection: pause when a pod comes out | AirPods with in-ear status in their broadcasts |
| Low battery notification | AirPods that report a level |

The draft also shows both settings for AirPods Max. That needs testing on a real pair, because the app ignores AirPods Max broadcasts today.

| Personalized Volume | AirPods 4, AirPods 4 (ANC), AirPods 5, Pro 2, Pro 3, Max 2 |

## Not checked per model

Apple's guide also covers these, but this list doesn't yet record which models support them.

- Spatial Audio and head tracking
- EQ
- Volume of sound effects
- Headphone accommodations
- Transparency mode customization
- Live Listen
